### What to know about the Iran war:
Iran said it targeted U.S. assets in Kuwait, Bahrain and Jordan and a vessel in the Strait of Hormuz on Tuesday, after the U.S. military said it carried out a 10th consecutive night of strikes on Iranian military infrastructure. There was no immediate confirmation of any U.S. bases in the region being hit.

The U.S. military released the name of an American soldier who was killed in Iraq during "a controlled detonation" of an Iranian drone over the weekend. The Pentagon previously identified two U.S. soldiers killed in an Iranian attack on an air base in Jordan, which left another listed as missing in action.
Nearly 100 American service members have been injured in Iranian strikes this month, U.S. officials told CBS News.
Defense Secretary Pete Hegseth told lawmakers the Iran war has cost about $37.5 billion.


### Canadian Tariffs:
The legality of recent U.S. tariffs on Canada is highly debated and currently subject to legal challenge.

The Recent Action: President Donald Trump announced 50% tariffs on various Canadian goods (set to take effect August 19, 2026) using Section 338 of the Tariff Act of 1930, a provision that had never previously been used. The White House cites alleged discrimination against U.S. automotive, alcohol, and dairy products.

Legal Debate: Legal experts question the authority of this 1930 law in light of subsequent trade legislation. 

Additionally, the U.S. Supreme Court ruled earlier in 2026 that the President exceeded emergency powers when enacting previous sweeping tariffs.

Broader Framework: Other sectoral tariffs on steel, aluminum, and lumber remain actively contested under international trade rules. You can review the details of these disputes on Congress.gov or track the legal analyses reported by CNBC.
